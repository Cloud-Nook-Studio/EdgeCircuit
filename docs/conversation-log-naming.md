### Names I would avoid

I would stay away from Cortex, Lucid, Praxis, Axiomera, and Scholar Forge. They already have meaningful use in software, technology, education, or adjacent businesses. “Cortex” is particularly crowded and is an Arm trademark used prominently in technology.

Peak, Focus, and anything using BrainHQ territory are also too close to existing cognitive-training products.

### My ranked shortlist

1. Mentavault — most distinctive and commercially expandable
2. Noetic Forge — strongest intellectual identity
3. Thesora — slickest standalone technology brand
4. Mental Capital — best executive positioning
5. Reasonwell — most trustworthy and accessible
6. Thesis One — best for the graduate-student audience

My recommendation would be:

**MENTAVAULT**  
*Cognitive training for consequential work.*

That gives you room to make the app much broader than puzzles. It could measure and train working memory, analytical speed, sustained focus, cognitive flexibility, verbal precision, decision-making under uncertainty, information synthesis, briefing recall and strategic prioritization.

### Important clearance step

The USPTO distinguishes trademarks from copyrights and provides the official federal trademark-search system. Before committing money to branding, you should search exact spellings, phonetic variants and similar meanings—not merely identical names.

For an app like this, the most relevant protection commonly includes downloadable software and potentially software or educational services. Similar marks in overlapping software classes can matter even when the products are not identical.

Best immediate choice: reserve Mentavault, Noetic Forge, and Thesora as finalists, then conduct a deeper USPTO, state-business, domain, app-store and social-handle clearance before selecting one.
