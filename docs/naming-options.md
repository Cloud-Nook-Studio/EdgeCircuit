# Naming Options

**Working date:** July 25, 2026  
**Selected working brand:** **EdgeCircuit**  
**Selected descriptor:** *Cognitive fitness for serious work.*  
**Status:** Owner-selected working brand; professional clearance remains
pending.

## Current decision

> **EdgeCircuit**  
> *Cognitive fitness for serious work.*

EdgeCircuit combines a performance-oriented outcome word with a repeatable
training-system metaphor. It supports the current **Daily Circuit** and the
broader language system recorded in
[EdgeCircuit Brand Architecture](brand-architecture.md).

This decision supersedes SharpMode as the working master brand. It does not
establish that EdgeCircuit is unregistered, registrable, or safe to use.

## Naming criteria

1. **Meaning:** signals active cognitive practice for demanding work.
2. **Distinctiveness:** memorable and plausibly protectable.
3. **Professional tone:** serious, premium, and non-clinical.
4. **Clarity:** pronounceable, spellable, and understandable.
5. **Extensibility:** supports circuits, stations, reviews, plans, and future
   products.
6. **Availability:** can survive trademark, domain, app-store, company-name,
   and social-handle clearance.

## Clearance disclaimer

**Nothing in this document constitutes legal advice or trademark clearance.**
Before public adoption, conduct a professional search covering similar
spellings, sounds, meanings, and commercial impressions across software, SaaS,
downloadable apps, and education/training services. Separately verify domains,
app stores, company registries, and social handles.
