# EdgeCircuit Brand Architecture

**Working date:** July 25, 2026  
**Status:** Canonical working brand architecture; professional name and
trademark clearance remain pending.

## Core brand

> **EdgeCircuit**  
> *Cognitive fitness for serious work.*

The main name stays distinctive and compact. The category descriptor provides
immediate clarity without making a medical, intelligence, or guaranteed
improvement claim.

### Other strong descriptors

- Professional cognitive training
- A daily workout for sharper thinking
- Mental conditioning for knowledge workers
- Train the capabilities behind great work
- Personalized cognitive performance training
- Cognitive conditioning for demanding work

The selected default remains:

> **EdgeCircuit**  
> *Cognitive fitness for serious work.*

## App Store naming

Keep the public brand clean while the store listing explains the product.

**Preferred listing:** **EdgeCircuit: Cognitive Fitness**

Other viable listing formats:

- EdgeCircuit: Brain Training
- EdgeCircuit: Mental Workouts
- EdgeCircuit: Focus & Memory
- EdgeCircuit: Cognitive Training

“Brain training” is immediately understandable but places the product closer to
legacy consumer-game brands. “Cognitive fitness” supports the intended
professional category.

## Positioning

### Conditioning, not generalized improvement

“Conditioning” communicates maintaining readiness and practicing under load
more credibly than vague promises of brain improvement.

> **EdgeCircuit**  
> *Cognitive conditioning for demanding work.*

This direction is especially relevant to executives, military professionals,
lawyers, physicians, researchers, and graduate students.

### Edge as a profile, not IQ

Potential future profile language:

- Current Edge
- Focus Edge
- Reasoning Edge
- Decision Edge
- Verbal Edge
- Memory Edge

Exercises may draw from established task domains such as fluid reasoning,
processing speed, working memory, spatial reasoning, and verbal reasoning.
Do not market their aggregate as IQ.

**EdgeScore is reserved, not active.** It may be introduced only after the
team defines what it measures, validates the aggregation, and completes a
claims review. Until then, the live product reports task-specific results and
uses Practice Charge only as a daily practice-quality meter.

### Audience-specific circuits

- **Executive Circuit** — prioritization, switching, and decision tasks
- **Scholar Circuit** — recall, synthesis, and reading-comprehension tasks
- **Briefing Circuit** — verbal precision and recall under time pressure
- **Deep Work Circuit** — sustained-attention and distraction-resistance tasks
- **Decision Circuit** — uncertainty, trade-offs, and calibration
- **Presentation Circuit** — retrieval, verbal fluency, and composure
- **Recovery Circuit** — lighter practice on low-readiness days

These are product-line concepts, not claims that the current six exercises
improve real-world performance in those domains.

### Performance-platform direction

The long-term product may build a personalized Daily Circuit and adapt task
difficulty from task-specific performance. Do not currently say EdgeCircuit
“measures cognitive readiness.” A safe present-tense formulation is:

> EdgeCircuit tracks performance on short cognitive exercises and adapts daily
> practice to the tasks completed.

## Working product narrative

The intended language sequence is:

> Complete your **Daily Circuit**.  
> Move through focused **Mental Sprints**.  
> Review task-specific performance.  
> Progress through the **Adaptive Loop**.

“Track your EdgeScore” remains excluded from live copy until the metric is
validated.

## Claims and clearance guardrails

- Never present EdgeCircuit as an IQ test, medical product, or validated
  measure of general cognitive readiness.
- Do not claim broad professional improvement from task practice.
- Keep performance feedback specific to the exercise played.
- Treat **EdgeCircuit** as a working brand until professional trademark,
  domain, app-store, company-name, and social-handle clearance is complete.
