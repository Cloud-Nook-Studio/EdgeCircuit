## Source handling

This is a distilled decision record rather than a verbatim transcript. The

## Conversation arc

The conversation first compared Mentavault with Cognivate. It concluded that
Cognivate should be eliminated because of active uses in neurorehabilitation,
technology consulting, and executive-oriented AI. Mentavault was considered
distinctive and expandable, but less immediate and energetic because “vault”
suggests storage more readily than active training.

The naming exploration then covered `menta`, `neuro`, `edge`, `sharp`, `focus`,
and unrelated professional-brand directions. The owner favored the **sharp**
theme and asked specifically about **Sharper** and **SharpCore**.

The conversation rejected:

- **Sharper** because an iPhone focus/productivity app already uses the name
  in adjacent territory, the term is highly descriptive, and it has an
  unfavorable older noun meaning.
- **SharpCore** because the exact name is used by a digital-services company
  and an open-source C# framework.
- **SharpSet**, **SharpFrame**, **SharpLevel**, **SharpGrade**, and
  **SharpEdge** because of existing uses, crowding, or weak ownership.

## Working decision

The strongest recommendation in the shared conversation was:

> **SharpMode**  
> *Cognitive training for serious work.*

Reasons preserved from the discussion:

- active rather than archival;
- modern, concise, and suitable for a daily app ritual;
- compatible with product language such as “Enter SharpMode,” “SharpMode
  session,” and “Focus Mode”;
- professional without sounding clinical or like a children’s puzzle game;
- no obvious exact-name brain-training, education, productivity, or
  consumer-software brand surfaced in that preliminary search.

The phrase appeared descriptively in an audio-equipment manual. That remote use
was not treated as inherently disqualifying, but it must remain in the
professional clearance scope.

## Product-language implications

Use **SharpMode** as the master brand, not as a claim that the product measures
general sharpness or intelligence. Appropriate extensions include:

- SharpMode session
- Focus Mode
- Recall Mode
- Reasoning Mode
- Practice Charge

Avoid **SharpMode Score** as a broad cognitive index until there is a validated
construct behind it. Current metrics remain exercise-specific, while Practice
Charge is an engagement and round-quality meter.

## Clearance status

**SharpMode is a working brand, not a cleared mark.** Before public launch or
material brand investment:

1. commission a professional similarity search, not only an exact-name search;
2. cover software, downloadable apps, SaaS, education, training, and related
   service categories;
3. search variants in sound, appearance, meaning, and commercial impression;
4. verify domains, app stores, business registries, and social handles; and
5. have qualified trademark counsel assess the final candidate.
